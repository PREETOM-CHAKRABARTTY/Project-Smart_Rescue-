## *Project description:*
<div align="justify">
Smart Rescue is a disaster management support system designed to assist rescuers and civilians during post-disaster situations. The project focuses on improving rescue efficiency by providing a digital platform for victim identification and emergency medical guidance. 

It has two features, first feature “Find Victims” helps identify victims by matching rescued individuals with missing person records, second feature “Quick medical guideline” provides basic emergency medical guidance for non-professional rescuers by evaluating a victim’s condition through symptom-based inputs and suggesting appropriate first-aid actions.
</div>
